# 🌊 RippleBot Cloud Hub

24/7 Discord Meeting Tracker & Voice Synthesis Service deployed on Hugging Face Spaces.

## Capabilities
- 🎙️ **DAVE Decryption & Voice Capture**: Real-time voice capture in `🔒 | Founders VC`.
- 🤖 **Groq Whisper & Qwen AI Summary**: Automated transcriptions and synthesis posted directly to `#📊｜meeting-reports`.
- ⏱️ **Attendee Statistics**: Accurate per-member hours and cumulative leaderboard in `meeting_stats.json`.
- 🔌 **Undeafened Gateway**: Joins without deafening so it actively receives audio packets.
- ⚡ **Auto-End Detection**: Automatically finalizes meeting after 60s of empty VC.
